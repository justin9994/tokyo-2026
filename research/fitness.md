### Tattoo / etiquette rules general note
- Tattoo bans are far more common and strictly enforced at **onsen/sento/spa facilities** than at commercial gyms (see Section 4). For gyms, policy is branch-dependent; bringing a rash guard or tattoo-cover tape is a reasonable hedge either way.
- **Indoor shoes**: most Japanese gyms (including municipal centers) require separate clean indoor training shoes — don't plan to wear your outdoor running shoes on gym floors; bring a spare pair or expect to buy/borrow.
- **Towel rental**: typically available for a small fee (¥100–300) at municipal centers and most private gyms; bring your own to save money/hassle.

## 3. Running routes from Hyatt Regency Tokyo (+ coffee pairing)

Late-Dec Tokyo mornings: ~2–5°C, dry, sunrise ~6:50am — good for early runs, dress in layers.

- **Shinjuku Chuo Park**: literally adjacent to the hotel (0 km) — small park, fine for a warm-up loop or shakeout jog, not a destination run.
- **Yoyogi Park + Meiji Jingu outer path**: ~2.5 km from the hotel. Yoyogi Park itself has an approx. 1.1 mi (1.75 km) loop path. → Coffee/breakfast: **Fuglen Tomigaya** (Nordic coffee bar, corner spot near the park, Tomigaya neighborhood just south of Meiji Shrine) or **Little Nap Coffee Stand** (5-chome-65-4 Yoyogi, directly opposite Yoyogi Park's main west entrance) or **Camelback Sandwich & Espresso** (well-known Tomigaya sandwich/coffee spot). Exact walking distance hotel→Yoyogi Park not independently confirmed this session but ~2.5 km / 30 min walk or one subway stop is a reasonable planning estimate.
- **Shinjuku Gyoen**: opens 9:00am (closed Mondays), admission **¥500** (some older sources cite ¥200 — expect ¥500 for 2026). **Running/jogging IS allowed but restricted**: only on the designated marked route, groups capped under 10 people, and jogging is banned entirely during cherry-blossom season (not an issue in Dec/Jan). A ~3.5 km loop course is popular with local runners, and the garden runs special early-morning openings on select days for runners. So the brief's assumption ("running NOT permitted") appears to be **outdated/incorrect** — verify current designated-route map at entry. Source: [Shinjuku Gyoen official garden rules](https://policies.env.go.jp/national-garden/shinjukugyoen/english/guide/rule/), [chocottrun.com](https://chocottrun.com/en/shinjuku-gyoen-course/).
- **Imperial Palace 5 km loop** (counter-clockwise is the local convention): classic loop, start/end near Hanzomon, Otemachi, Nijubashimae, Hibiya, or Sakuradamon stations. Two runner-support facilities near the course:
  - **adidas Runbase**: Hirakawacho Mori Tower near Nagatacho Stn — showers + ~250 lockers, shoe rental, gear shop. Locker+shower **¥800**, towel rental **¥200**. [metropolisjapan.com/adidas-runbase](https://metropolisjapan.com/adidas-runbase/)
  - **Run Pit**: Palace Side Building above Takebashi Station — lockers/showers for palace-loop runners (fee not confirmed this session).
  - Access via Marunouchi or Toei Oedo Line to Sakuradamon as suggested in the brief is reasonable — both lines serve stations near the palace moat.
  - → Coffee in Marunouchi after: **Common** and **Saza Coffee KITTE** were named in the brief; not independently re-verified this session but both are real, known Marunouchi-area coffee spots near Tokyo Station/KITTE.
- **Meguro River path → Onibus Coffee Nakameguro / Sidewalk Stand**: real, well-known Nakameguro running/cafe pairing; distance from Hyatt Regency Shinjuku not verified this session (expect a taxi or ~20 min train ride — this is not walkable from Shinjuku, treat it as a separate outing rather than a morning-run add-on unless you take the train down first).
- **Komazawa Olympic Park**: 2.1 km loop course, popular running park from the 1964 Olympics; further from Shinjuku (Setagaya) — plan as a train/taxi trip, not a run-from-hotel route.
- **Sumida River / Asakusa → Fuglen Asakusa**: Fuglen has an Asakusa location; Sumida River path is a legitimate flat riverside running route. Asakusa is far from Shinjuku (different side of the city) — again best treated as a standalone excursion, not a hotel-adjacent run.

**Not independently re-verified this session, budget-limited**: precise walk/run distances hotel→Yoyogi Park, hotel→Imperial Palace, and confirmation of Run Pit's fee — treat estimates above as planning-grade, sanity-check with Google Maps closer to the trip.
