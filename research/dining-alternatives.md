## Ranked list

### 1. Narukiyo なるきよ: the benchmark
- **Where and how far:** B1F, 2-7-14 Shibuya (VORT Aoyama / Nakamura Bldg), between Shibuya and Omotesando.
  - From the Hyatt: about 20 min by taxi, or about 25 min by JR to Shibuya plus an 8-min walk.
  - From Shimokitazawa: about 15 min on the Inokashira line to Shibuya.
- **Style:** Chef Narukiyo Yoshida's underground izakaya. Kyushu fish, handwritten specials, music, a fashion and creative crowd. 25 seats. Groups of 4 or more may be put in the zashiki (tatami room).
- **Price:**
  - TableEX omakase courses, food only: ¥8,800, ¥11,000, ¥13,200 or ¥16,500, plus a ¥1,000 booking fee.
  - Tabelog average spend: ¥10–15k.
  - Plan on **¥15–22k per person with sake**.
- **Hours:** Mon–Sat 18:00–00:00 (some sources say 00:30). **Closed Sundays.**
- **New Year:** **Unverified.** No 年末年始 notice on Tabelog, Instagram or in search.
  - The TableEX request calendar blocks only Sundays (and Dec 4), and lets you pick Jan 1–2, so it **does not reflect the holiday closure**.
  - Assume it is closed about Dec 31–Jan 3 or 4. Jan 4 is the riskiest of the three weeknights, and Jan 6 or 7 are safer.
- **Booking:**
  - **TableEX** (https://table-ex.com/restaurants/187) is a *request* service, and Justin is already logged in. The rule is "reservations accepted starting one month prior". The calendar already lets you pick January dates, but expect it to be confirmed only once the window opens: ~Dec 4 for Jan 4, ~Dec 6 for Jan 6, ~Dec 7 for Jan 7. "Instant Reservation" is "coming soon".
  - **Phone** 03-5485-2223. A sub-agent saw a Tabelog note that phone lines are closed and to book online, but I couldn't confirm that.
  - Hyatt concierge: no evidence they book it. TableEX is the cleaner route.
- **Live availability:** none visible. TableEX only shows a request calendar.

### 2. Kanae 鼎: Shinjuku 3-chome sake izakaya (Dec 28, Jan 7, Jan 6)
- **How far:** about 15 min (Marunouchi line Nishi-Shinjuku → Shinjuku-sanchome, or an 8-min taxi).
- **Style:** Basement Showa-era folk-house izakaya, open since 1972. Tabelog 3.58, Izakaya Hyakumeiten. Deep sake list and serious fish. 60 seats, with a semi-private room that fits exactly 4.
- **Price:** about ¥6–10k (Tabelog spend ¥10–15k with drinking), plus a ¥550 charge.
- **Hours:** Mon–Sat 16:00–23:30 (food last order 22:30). Holidays until 23:00. Closed Sundays.
- **New Year:** Tabelog standing note says **closed Dec 31–Jan 5**. Its booking calendar shows Jan 1–5 blocked, which matches.
- **Booking:** Tabelog online (https://tabelog.com/tokyo/A1304/A130401/13000879/), or phone 03-3352-7646.
- **LIVE (Tabelog widget, 4 people ○):**
  - **Dec 28:** 16:00–18:00 and 20:30, 20:45, 21:00, 21:30.
  - **Jan 6 and Jan 7:** 16:00–21:30.
- **Note:** Dec 28 is peak bonenkai season. Book a seat rather than relying on walk-in.

### 3. Shinjuku Kappo Nakajima 新宿割烹 中嶋 (Jan 7 if no DisneySea)
- **How far:** about 15 min (Shinjuku-sanchome).
- **Style:** Michelin-pedigree kappo. Fourth-generation chef, famous owan. The calmer, more "chef's table" counterpoint to Narukiyo.
- **Price:** ¥15,730 per person (Pocket Concierge omakase course plus a toast drink). Plan about ¥20–25k with drinks.
- **Hours:** dinner 17:30–21:00 (last order 20:00). **Closed Sun, Mon and holidays**, so Dec 28 and Jan 4 are out.
- **New Year:** unverified. The old pattern was Dec 28–Jan 6, so Jan 7 would be the first day back. The Pocket Concierge calendar *does* offer Jan 7.
- **Booking:** Pocket Concierge (https://www.pocket-concierge.jp/en/restaurants/243774), which is request-style, or phone 03-3356-4534.
- **LIVE:** Jan 7, 4 guests: **17:30, 18:00, 18:30, 19:00** offered, private room (no room charge).
