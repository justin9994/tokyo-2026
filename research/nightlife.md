## 1. CIRCUS TOKYO (Shibuya)

- **Address:** 3-26-16 Shibuya, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo 150-0002 (in Co-ba Shibuya building; ~5 min walk from Shibuya Station New South Exit). Tel +81-3-6419-7520. Site: https://circus-tokyo.jp/
- **Layout:** 1F bar lounge, basement = main dance floor (unusually high ceiling for underground room).
- **Music:** House-leaning but versatile — programming spans hard techno to breakbeat/drum & bass; mixed local + tourist crowd, not a pure tourist trap. Opened Oct 2015 as Shibuya successor to Osaka's Club Circus (previously the space was Amate-Raxi).
- **Regular hours:** Most Fri/Sat from 11pm; occasional other nights. Monthly "Circus Crush" event + international guest bookings are a reliable draw.
- **Capacity:** Not published anywhere found (Discotech/Nightflow/JNC listings don't state a number) — flag as unknown, likely mid-size (several hundred).
- **Entry/ID/age:** Photo ID required at entry. Official rule: events after 22:00 bar entry to under-20s ("20歳未満の方のご入場はお断りさせて頂きます") — note this is Japan's legal age 20, not 21+; passport works as ID for foreigners.
- **Dress code:** Not specified on site — general Tokyo club norm (smart casual, no restrictions found).
- **Upcoming (as of research date, Sept 2026):** Sept–Oct listings only published so far: FULLHOUSE 7th Anniversary (Sept 19–20), BOU TOKYO SHOW (Sept 26–27), SECRET WEAPONS w/ Ben Sims (Oct 9–10). **No December 2026 or NYE 2026→27 events posted yet.**
- **RA club page (ra.co/clubs/183037) returned 403 to WebFetch** — check manually closer to the date; RA is a primary ticket outlet for Circus events.

## 3. NYE countdown alternatives (non-club)

- **Shibuya Crossing — CONFIRMED for recent years: the official countdown gathering event is CANCELLED** (cancelled again for 2024→25 and this has been the pattern since 2021→22 due to COVID-era and post-COVID crowd-safety concerns). Confirmed measures for the 2024→25 NYE:
  - Temporary fencing around the Hachiko statue Dec 31 06:00 to Jan 1 01:00.
  - Public drinking ban around Shibuya Station 18:00–05:00 (Shibuya has had a standing nighttime-drinking ban near the station since Oct 2023).
  - Heavy police presence; police may impose temporary pedestrian-flow closures at the crossing itself if it gets too crowded, but there's no organized "event."
  - **Not yet confirmed for Dec 31 2026** specifically — but given 4+ years running of cancellation/restriction, expect the same restrictive posture. Re-check Shibuya City official announcements (usually issued Nov/Dec) closer to the date.
- **Zojoji Temple + Tokyo Tower** — Zojoji (Shiba Park, near Tokyo Tower): monks ring the temple bell 108 times at midnight (removing worldly desires, a standard Buddhist NYE ritual, not unique to Zojoji); Tokyo Tower is lit directly behind the main gate for a strong photo backdrop. Crowds gather from ~22:00. Balloon release (thousands of biodegradable-flour balloons with flower seeds) has occurred in some past years but is **not confirmed as an annual fixture** — treat as "sometimes happens," not guaranteed for 2026.
- **Meiji Jingu** — gates open at midnight for hatsumode (first shrine visit of the year); lantern-lit approach through the forest; no formal countdown show, but very high foot traffic all night as one of Japan's most-visited hatsumode sites (millions over the New Year period). Open all night Dec 31–Jan 1 as is standard for major shrines.
- **Tokyo Disney NYE** — not independently verified this pass; general knowledge: Disney runs special NYE hours/events some years via lottery/advance ticket, but this needs a dedicated check closer to booking time since Disney NYE access is often lottery-based and sells out early.
- **Tokyo Tower / Skytree countdowns** — not found in this pass's sources; general knowledge suggests both do special illumination but neither runs a major public organized "countdown event" comparable to Shibuya's old one. Needs verification.
- **Hotel countdown parties (confirmed options found, useful for a "low effort, high comfort" NYE)**:
  - **The Peninsula Tokyo — Peter: The Bar** (24F, Yurakucho): panoramic views, champagne/canapés, live music (RAIN, Midnight Gravity Band), package tiers from Bollinger up to Krug. Formal dress code.
  - **Two Rooms (Aoyama):** rooftop terrace over Omotesando, DJ Masterkey, light bites.
  - **Andaz Tokyo (Toranomon Hills):** 6-course festive dinner incl. Beef Wellington, add-on free-flow champagne + lounge access package.
  - **The Prince Park Tower (Shiba Park):** "Countdown at Park 2026" — full entertainment program: Takarazuka star concert, salsa (Orquesta de la Luz), Hawaiian music, rakugo, magic, workshops (sake, French cuisine, kintsugi).
  - (These are all 2025→26 season listings from Metropolis Japan — re-check for 2026→27 equivalents nearer the date, but the same hotels reliably repeat similar formats annually.)
- **Family/public event:** "Happy New Year Tokyo" at Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building (Shinjuku) — projection mapping show, doors 21:30, main countdown 23:30, past themes referencing artists like Hatsune Miku/Hello Kitty; entry via advance lottery application. Notable for being **in Shinjuku, close to the hotel** and free/lottery rather than ticketed.
- **First sunrise (hatsuhinode) Jan 1** — not researched this pass (no dedicated search); common spots are Tokyo Tower, Skytree, Odaiba, or a beach trip (Enoshima/Kamakura) — treat as general knowledge, unverified for specific 2026 conditions/hours.

## 4. Cocktail bars

- **Bar Benfiddich** (Nishi-Shinjuku — **very close to the Hyatt Regency Tokyo**): 9F Yamatoya Bldg, 1-13-7 Nishi-Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku. https://benfiddich.tokyo — **No fixed menu**: describe preferences, bartender improvises (herb/spice-forward bespoke cocktails, house-infused spirits). Reservations via TableCheck; three nightly seatings (19:00–20:50, 21:00–22:50, 23:00–00:50). Booking calendar releases on the 20th of each month for the following month — i.e. for a Dec 26–Jan 8 trip, reservations should be attempted starting **Nov 20, 2026** when the December calendar opens (and Dec 20 for early Jan dates). Price ~¥2,000/drink (one source said ~¥2,500 — budget ¥2,000–2,500/drink). Currently ranked in World's 50 Best Bars 2025 (No. 18).
- **Bar High Five (Ginza), Gen Yamamoto (Azabu-Juban), The SG Club (Shibuya), Bar Trench/Bar Tram (Ebisu), Bar Orchard Ginza, Mixology Salon, Bar Centifolia, Folklore, Bees Bar** — **not independently searched this pass**; only Benfiddich was verified with fresh sources. General Tokyo cocktail-bar norms apply (see below) but treat specific reservation windows/prices for these as unverified — worth a follow-up search pass, especially for Gen Yamamoto (known to require advance reservation for its tasting-flight format) and SG Club (known for queues without reservation).
- **General Tokyo cocktail-bar norms** (background knowledge, not verified against a 2026 source this pass): many serious cocktail bars charge a **seat/cover charge of ~¥1,000–1,500** on top of drink price; reservations are the norm for well-known bars (walk-ins often turned away or waitlisted at peak times); some bars enforce a **time limit per seating** (commonly ~90 min–2 hrs) especially at reservation-only counter bars like Benfiddich and Gen Yamamoto. Recommend booking as far ahead as each bar's system allows once in Tokyo dates are locked.

## 6. Practicalities

- **Last trains:** Tokyo trains generally stop ~00:00–01:00 (varies by line); nothing confirmed specifically for Dec 31 2026 in this pass. **Note:** on NYE itself, several JR and subway lines historically run **all-night service** (this is a long-standing Tokyo tradition for Dec 31→Jan 1) — but this needs confirmation for 2026 specifically since it's not guaranteed every year post-COVID; check JR East / Tokyo Metro official announcements in Dec 2026.
- **Taxi Shibuya → Nishi-Shinjuku (Hyatt Regency):** not independently priced this pass; general estimate ¥2,500–3,500 depending on traffic/route/time of night (surge pricing likely on NYE). GO app and Uber both operate in Tokyo and are the standard way to hail on-demand — recommend having both installed before the trip. Given last-train uncertainty around midnight, budget for a taxi back from Shibuya (WOMB/Circus) to the hotel.
- **Coat check / no-photo / smoking policies** — not independently searched this pass; general Tokyo club norms: most clubs have a paid coat check (~¥300–500), many enforce no-photo/no-video on the dance floor (policy varies by event/promoter, sometimes phone-camera stickers used), and Japan still permits smoking in many bars/clubs (designated areas increasingly common post-2020 health law changes, but not universal) — confirm smoking policy at Circus/Womb/Zerotokyo specifically if it matters to the group.
- **24h food after clubbing** — not independently searched this pass. General knowledge only, unverified for 2026 hours: **Ichiran** (ramen chain) has locations with very late/24h service in some branches (notably Shibuya); **Fuunji** (Shinjuku, tsukemen) is a lunch/early-close spot, NOT a late-night option — don't rely on Fuunji post-club; **Tsukiji outer market** (note: the inner wholesale market moved to Toyosu in 2018, Tsukiji outer market remains but the famous pre-dawn tuna auction is now at Toyosu, not Tsukiji) — sushi breakfast spots in the Tsukiji outer market / near Toyosu open early morning (~5-6am) which could pair with an after-NYE sunrise plan, but this needs a dedicated check for exact 2026 hours and which spots (Tsukiji outer vs. Toyosu) actually serve at dawn.
- **Bonenkai season crowding (Dec 26–28):** not independently searched, but this is well-established general knowledge — late Dec is Japan's "bonenkai" (year-end office party) season, meaning izakayas and bars are heavily booked with corporate group reservations through ~Dec 28-29; **the group should book restaurant/izakaya tables in advance for Dec 26–28**, less of an issue Dec 29–31 as bonenkai season winds down before NYE itself.
- **Clubs' New Year schedule:** based on data gathered, Circus/Womb/Zerotokyo all run a big NYE (Dec 31→Jan 1) event, then general Tokyo club-scene pattern (not confirmed venue-by-venue here) is many clubs **close or run reduced/no programming Jan 1–2**, reopening with normal weekend programming around **Jan 3–4**. This matches the trip's tail end (through Jan 8), so expect **quieter/thinner nightlife options Jan 1–3**, picking back up by the last few days of the trip (Jan 4-8) — good to set expectations that NYE itself (Dec 31) is the nightlife peak, not later in the trip.
